## Slide 7
Planned speaking time: 75 seconds. Audience: plant breeders.

DM is dry matter yield; FW is fresh weight; MC is moisture content. RAD-seq and UNEAK produced the source marker dataset. After QC 13,687 SNPs remained; 11,901 contributed to the prediction relationship matrix because of input coding. A correctly coded 13,687-marker matrix gave off-diagonal r=0.999 and changed predictive ability by at most 0.033. Ploidy-specific dosage was unavailable.

Stage one uses site-specific mixed models to obtain genotype BLUEs. Population group and genotype nested within group are fixed; design effects are random. Stage two uses BGLR with the three genomic prediction models. Population group is fixed in the main prediction analyses.

## Slide 9
Planned speaking time: 90 seconds. Audience: plant breeders.

M1 has a non-marker genotype effect; M2 adds a genomic effect using the relationship matrix; M3 adds genomic by environment covariance, constructed with a Hadamard product. Population group is fixed in the main prediction analysis, but random for the variance-component illustration.

Source: Lubanga et al. · supplied manuscript

## Slide 10
Planned speaking time: 105 seconds. Audience: plant breeders.

Five-fold cross-validation is used. CV1 masks the whole genotype across environments. CV2 retains records of the target genotype elsewhere. Site-year CV2 can use another year of the same plants at the same site, so it does not establish prediction in a new environment.

Source: Lubanga et al. · supplied manuscript

## Slide 11
Planned speaking time: 105 seconds. Audience: plant breeders.

Each design has 25 measurements per environment and 125 in total. OL genotypes are measured in all five environments and NOL genotypes once. Each design was independently replicated ten times. Increasing OL simultaneously reduces unique training genotypes; the series cannot identify a universal optimum overlap fraction.

Source: Manuscript · Table 1

## Slide 12
Planned speaking time: 135 seconds. Audience: plant breeders.

Combine years into one BLUE per genotype per site. In each partition subtract trial by population-group means estimated from training data only; add the training means back to predictions. At evaluation centre both predicted and observed values by group to calculate the pooled within-group correlation. This is not an average of three group-specific correlations. Five folds and ten repetitions use identical partitions. Trial-level CV2 uses the other site, removing the same-site repeated-year information.

## Slide 22
Planned speaking time: 90 seconds. Audience: plant breeders.

The study addresses phenotype measurement allocations rather than proven reductions in plot establishment or maintenance costs. The most direct application is reducing measurement frequency at established perennial trials. It does not justify removing annual harvest management or assume completely new environments can be predicted.
